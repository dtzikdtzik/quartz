# Spells

- [Mending](https://www.thievesguild.cc/spells/spell?spellid=873) gains additional text:
###### **AT HIGHER LEVELS**
If the target is a construct creature, it regains 1d4 HP for each spell slot level. Additionally, the casting time for mending is reduced to 1 action.

# Species

- [Warforged](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/species:warforged) cannot eat or drink. They cannot gain effects or benefits from potions or other items requiring organic ingestion.
